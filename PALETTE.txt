AquaTrace FINAL HOD/Conference UI palette.

Design intent:
- Dark navy/slate foundation for scientific instrumentation context.
- Muted blue, green, red, orange, mustard yellow and plum accents.
- Polymer cards use one consistent geometry and layout; only the accent colour changes by polymer.
- Confidence rings are identical in size, track, border and typography across all cards.
- KPI cards use consistent geometry with five restrained accent colours.
- Guidance cards and status elements reuse the same palette system.
- No neon colours, fluorescent glow, luminous gradients, or excessive glow effects.
- Real Firebase/current-session data logic is unchanged.
