CITIZEN GUIDANCE

Understand microplastic screening

A public-facing guide for households, schools, community groups and people using freshwater sources.

H₂O
01

What an AquaTrace result means

AquaTrace is designed as a screening system. A polymer label is the class suggested by the current machine-learning model, while confidence represents the model's output for that prediction.

A screening result is not the same as laboratory-certified polymer concentration or regulatory confirmation.
02

Reduce plastic reaching water

  • Use reusable bottles, bags and containers where practical.
  • Keep plastic waste out of drains, lakes, rivers and streams.
  • Separate plastic waste for the collection system available in your area.
  • Do not burn plastic waste or dispose of it into open water.
03

Remember synthetic fibres

Textiles made from synthetic polymers can release small fibres during use and washing. Good laundry and wastewater practices can help reduce the amount reaching the environment.

04

Protect sampling points

Keep sample containers clean, avoid touching the inside surfaces, and follow a consistent sampling procedure so repeated tests are easier to compare.

05
